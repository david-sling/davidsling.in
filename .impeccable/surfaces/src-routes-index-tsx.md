# Landing (/)

**Visitor mode:** Experience. The work shows within the first scroll.

- **Audience:** developer peers first, then future collaborators, then hiring evaluators.
- **Job:** decide within about 30 seconds that David builds real things with taste.
- **Action:** open a project page, read a post, or copy the email.
- **Proof:** real media only:
  - Wave's demo GIF.
  - Equanim's pendulum running live.
  - Offlyn's app images.
  - Repo facts.
- **Constraints:** a short index. Depth lives on the project pages.
- **Signature moment:** the hero headline staggers in once. After that, every clickable element answers with tactile feedback.
- **Open:** the project pages and blog pages are shaped separately.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The familiar personal portfolio (the category standard, chosen by David), executed at spell.sh-level interaction craft. It refuses concept costumes and the bought-template sameness of loose spacing, stock cards and dead buttons.

**OWN-WORLD:**
- **Ground:** a soft neutral with large rounded sheet sections, no cream.
- **Ink:** near-black.
- **Accent:** David's blue `#67D0E8`, used as a fill with ink text on it. A deeper shade of the same hue is used for text-level accents.
- **Type:** General Sans throughout, with two-tone headlines.
- **Components:** pill buttons with sliding arrows, tilted sticker tags, and raised logo tiles.

**STORY:** The visitor sees who David is and what he claims (an engineer with a designer's eye). They scan three real projects, see how he builds with agents, check his roles and writing, then click through or say hi.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:**
- **Header:** the wordmark on the left. On the right, the Work, Writing and About links and the email pill with copy.
- **Hero:** a centred sheet holding David's cut-out portrait with a tilted name sticker, then a two-line headline of about 64–88px.
- **Actions:** the primary "See the work" button and a secondary "Read the writing" button.
- **Below the fold:** the top of the Selected Work cards peeks in.

**FORM:** The canon (the standing exit), seed:6226814c5bfd363d.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
